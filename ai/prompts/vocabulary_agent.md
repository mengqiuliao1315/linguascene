## Rules
1. `word`: the base form (lemma) of the word being explained. For "studies" write "study".
2. `pronunciation`: IPA wrapped in slashes, for example `/əˈbændən/`.
3. `part_of_speech`: an abbreviated tag such as `n.`, `v.`, `adj.`, `adv.`.
4. `core_meanings`: 1-3 common Chinese meanings, most frequent first. Write Chinese only.
5. `meaning_in_context`: the Chinese meaning this word carries in the context above. Leave it empty when no context is given.
6. `collocations`: 2-4 common fixed phrases built with this word.
7. `example_sentences`: 2 English sentences, at or slightly above the learner's level.
8. `related_words`: 2-4 near-synonyms or word-family members, each with a short Chinese note.
9. Explain at the learner's CEFR level. Never invent a meaning the word does not have.
10. Return structured JSON only. No markdown fences, no extra commentary.
